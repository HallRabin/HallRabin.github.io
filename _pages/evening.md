<center><h3>We’re so excited for you to join us!</h3></center>
<br>

#### Travel and Timings

Please arrive at Batch Country House from approx 6.30pm. That means you’ll be in time to grab a quick drink before the all-important first dance and opening of the dance floor! If using public transport, you should catch the train to Weston-super-Mare and take a taxi to the reception venue (~15 mins). See [here](/accommodation) for information about accomodation.

#### Food

Pizza will be served at around 9pm - they will all be vegetarian and some vegan! The cake will be cut just before the first dance (around 7pm).

#### Other

If you want to make the most of your trip, you can spend the day in Weston-super-Mare as a classic English seaside town. Bristol is the closest city (~45 min drive, or 20-30 min train from Weston-super-Mare) and is worth visiting if you haven't been before.
